User Data Deletion Instructions

In compliance with Google Play Store and Apple App Store user data policies, as well as GDPR and CCPA regulations, Howilook gives users full autonomy to delete their personal data and photos at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Account & Data Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the Howilook application on your mobile device.
  2. Navigate to Settings (gear icon in the bottom navigation or profile).
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Settings screen.
  4. Tap Delete Account (marked in red).
  5. Confirm the prompt by tapping DELETE ACCOUNT PERMANENTLY.

Upon confirmation, our backend executes an automated cascade deletion that permanently purges:

  • Your user profile, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • All outfit and face scan score records.
  • All stored images and avatars from cloud storage buckets.
  • Payment records and referral reward linkages.

Method 2: Email Request

If you have already uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you can request manual data deletion by sending an email from your registered email address to:

Email: privacy@howilook.app
Subject: Account & Data Deletion Request

Our team will verify your identity and permanently delete all data associated with your account within 30 days of receiving your request.
