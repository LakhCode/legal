Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 3, 2026

Your privacy is of foundational importance to Howilook. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, protect, and delete your personal data when using our application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Email address, display name, and avatar URL provided via Google Sign-In or manual account creation.
  • Photos & Visual Data: Selfies and outfit photos captured through your device camera or uploaded from your photo library.
  • Aesthetic & Style Preferences: Style goals, aesthetic choices, and physical dimensions (height, weight, body type) that you voluntarily supply to personalize styling accuracy.
  • Transaction Records: Payment IDs and plan tiers processed via secure payment providers (we do not store raw credit card numbers or banking passwords).

2. How We Use Visual & Biometric Data

When you request an outfit or face scan:

  • Images are transferred over TLS-encrypted channels to secure backend processors (Google Cloud / Vertex AI).
  • The AI extracts visual aesthetic indicators (such as color coordination, fit, grooming, and facial symmetry) to formulate recommendations.
  • No Facial Recognition Database: We do not use your photos to build persistent facial identification templates or cross-reference your face against third-party law enforcement, advertising, or facial recognition databases.
  • Private Encrypted Storage: User images are stored in private, access-controlled storage buckets inaccessible to other users or web crawlers.

3. Data Retention & User Rights

  • On-Demand Scan Deletion: You can delete any individual scan from your history screen at any time; this purges both the database assessment and the underlying storage photo.
  • Complete Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your entire account, all associated scan data, profiles, and photos directly inside Settings → Delete Account.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with vetted enterprise infrastructure providers who comply with stringent privacy standards:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL & Storage): Secure database, authentication, and encrypted object storage.
  • Google Cloud (Vertex AI): Cloud-based machine learning inference.
  • Razorpay: PCI-DSS compliant payment processing.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information or photographs of children.

6. Contact Us

For questions or data deletion requests, contact our Data Protection team at privacy@howilook.app.
