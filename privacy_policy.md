# Privacy Policy for Slang Book

**Effective Date: April 16, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the Slang Book mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect

When you use the App, we collect information in the following ways:

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Account Information:** If you choose to log in using Google Sign-In, we collect basic profile information provided by Google, such as your name and email address, to create and manage your account.
- **Payment Information:** If you make in-app purchases (e.g., Premium subscription), your payment details are processed securely by our third-party payment gateway, Razorpay. We do not store your full credit card numbers or sensitive billing details on our servers.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
Like many apps, we automatically collect certain information about your device and how you interact with the App, which helps us improve the user experience.
- **Log and Usage Data:** Information about your interactions with the App, such as features used and time spent.
- **Device Information:** Data such as your device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers (e.g., Firebase installation IDs or advertising identifiers).
- **Crash and Performance Data:** Diagnostics to help us identify and fix bugs (via Firebase Crashlytics).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- **Authentication and Account Management:** To create, manage, and authenticate your user account via Google Sign-In.
- **Service Provision:** To provide the core functionality of the App, including saving your preferences or premium status.
- **Payment Processing:** To facilitate transactions and verify premium access.
- **Analytics and Improvement:** To understand how users interact with the App and to improve its features and performance (via Firebase Analytics).
- **Advertising:** To serve advertisements (via Google AdMob). Ad networks may use device identifiers to personalize marketing content.

## 3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party service providers to help operate our App. These providers have access to your data only to perform tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.
- **Firebase (Google LLC):** Used for analytics, crash reporting, databases (Firestore), and authentication. 
  - [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Razorpay:** Used for secure payment processing.
  - [Razorpay Privacy Policy](https://razorpay.com/privacy/)

## 4. Advertising and Analytics Choices

- **Personalized Advertising:** Depending on your device settings and location (e.g., if you are in the EEA/UK), you may be prompted to consent to personalized advertising. You can opt-out of personalized ads at any time through your device's settings (e.g., "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android, or "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS).

## 5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information. However, please be aware that no method of electronic transmission or data storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App contains a slang dictionary with potentially sensitive or mature terms and is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete the information.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
